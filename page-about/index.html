About Us

Passion, Excellence & Celebration

Bio — Founding Director
Rutendo Sithole-Gwatidzo
The Founder of Rutie's Group is a passionate entrepreneur with a deep love for both corporate and retail events. Since establishing the business in 2014, she has successfully grown Rutie's Group into a trusted brand known for creativity, professionalism, and exceptional service. With years of experience in event coordination, party supplies, corporate gifting, and décor solutions, she is dedicated to bringing memorable experiences to life for every client. Her commitment to excellence, attention to detail, and passion for people continue to drive the company's success and reputation in the industry.

Our Journey

Rutie's Group started because we wanted to make fun parties AND help businesses look good. We began as a tiny party shop and grew into two big parts. Rutie's Party Shop for regular folks and our Corporate Division for businesses. We have a real store at Fourways Mall where you can get everything for parties: balloons, decorations, themed stuff, you name it. And for businesses, we do custom printing, fancy company gifts, team uniforms, branded cups and mugs, and executive gifts all over South Africa.

We celebrate with you during birthdays, weddings, business events, anything. Customers first, creativity always. We make your moment shine, no matter what.

Our Vision

To help Africa celebrate better. Great parties for families, great merch for businesses. Simple.

Our Mission

To make your parties and business stuff awesome. We give you great party supplies, amazing decorations, and custom printed products all done with honesty, heart, and getting every little thing right.
